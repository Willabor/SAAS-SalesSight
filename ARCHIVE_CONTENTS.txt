REAL DATA EXAMPLES
==================

Style 8501B (Argonaut Nations - Men's Jeans):
- Pack A: Small sizes (4× 30W, 2× 32W, 2× 34W, ...) = 12 pieces
- Pack B: Large sizes (2× 40W, 2× 42W, 1× 44W, ...) = 12 pieces
- Pack C: Balanced distribution = 12 pieces
- Pack E: Extended sizes (4× 44W, 4× 46W, 4× 48W) = 12 pieces
         ONLY 3 colors: Black, Grey, White (demonstrates color override)

All packs: $300.00 per box = $25.00 per unit

CRITICAL LESSONS LEARNED
=========================

1. Prepacks are COLOR-SPECIFIC (one color per box)
2. Cost calculation must prioritize specified style first
3. Related styles need fallback search
4. Vendor type UX needs clear radio buttons, not toggle
5. Hybrid vendors need special documentation
6. Pack E demonstrates color override (3 colors vs 14)
